C2

Housing First Navigator

Cutting through bureaucratic complexity so that finding a home takes hours, not months.

TEAM PHOTO — PLACEHOLDER
THEME

Project Theme

Access to Stable Housing — dismantling the administrative barriers that keep people out of available units even when those units exist.

PROBLEM

Problem Statement

Navigating the patchwork of housing applications, waitlists, and eligibility criteria is overwhelming for anyone, let alone someone in crisis. Applications are paper-heavy, scattered across dozens of agencies, and require documentation that unhoused individuals rarely have on hand.

WHY

Motivation

The Housing First model has a strong evidence base — stable housing first, then support services. Yet the practical path into housing is so complex that many people give up before they reach the threshold. We wanted to collapse that distance to a manageable few steps.

CHALLENGE

Biggest Challenge

Government systems are fragmented with no shared API or data standard. Digital literacy varies widely among the people we serve, and the lowest-friction design for one group created barriers for another. Finding a universal baseline was painstaking.

SOLUTION

Proposed Solution

A guided, step-by-step digital navigator that aggregates housing resources from multiple agencies, auto-fills common application fields from a single intake profile, and tracks progress through each open application. Available as a web app and via SMS for low-data users.

OUTCOME

Outcome

Functional MVP built and tested with 25 end-users over eight weeks. Twelve successful housing application submissions facilitated. Average time-to-first-submission reduced from 11 days to under 3 hours in pilot testing.

SDGs

Target SDG Goals

SDG 1 · No Poverty SDG 10 · Reduced Inequalities SDG 11 · Sustainable Cities
LESSON

Most Important Lesson Learned

Simplicity is everything. Every extra step in a form, every extra field, is a door that might not open. The most impactful design decisions were the things we removed — not the features we added.

FUTURE

Future Direction

Negotiate a data-sharing agreement with the city housing authority for real-time vacancy tracking. Add a document-scanning feature so users can digitise required ID from a phone camera. Translate the navigator into five languages spoken most commonly in shelter populations.
