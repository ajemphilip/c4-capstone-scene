Seven organizations who brought the work out of the classroom and into the community — providing access, expertise, and the honest friction that makes solutions real.

PARTNER 01

HICC

HOUSING CRISIS SUPPORT YORK REGION

The Homelessness & Housing in Crisis Centre (HICC) is a leading hub for coordinated homelessness response in the York Region. HICC manages the region’s by-name list, operates warming and overnight programmes, and serves as the connective tissue between emergency services and permanent housing pathways. Their data infrastructure and frontline expertise grounded the entire C4 cohort in the real conditions of homelessness in our local context.

PARTNER 02

Youth Services Bureau

YOUTH MENTAL HEALTH HOUSING

Youth Services Bureau (YSB) is one of the region’s largest providers of mental health, housing, and employment services for young people aged 12–29. YSB supported C4’s work by providing access to youth advisory panels, frontline workers who understood the complexity of youth homelessness, and programme spaces for user testing. Their ethos — meeting young people where they are without preconditions — shaped the design principles across multiple teams.

PARTNER 03

BetterStreet

TECHNOLOGY RESOURCE NAVIGATION DATA

BetterStreet is a community resource navigation platform focused on making local services visible, searchable, and accurate for people in need. As a technology partner, BetterStreet provided C4 teams with live data infrastructure insights, an understanding of the technical and organizational challenges of maintaining real-time service directories, and a model for what responsible community data stewardship looks like in practice. Their collaboration was central to the C3 team’s Resource Access Portal.

PARTNER 04

Trek For Teens

YOUTH MENTORSHIP RESILIENCE

Trek For Teens provides experiential and therapeutic outdoor programmes for at-risk youth, building resilience, self-efficacy, and community belonging through shared challenge. Their approach — which centres relationship over curriculum — informed the C4 and C6 teams’ thinking about how connection and trust form the invisible infrastructure of recovery. Trek For Teens also helped the cohort understand how to design programmes that are genuinely accessible to young people experiencing homelessness or housing instability.

PARTNER 05

360° Kids

YOUTH HOMELESSNESS SHELTER OUTREACH

360° Kids is a York Region organization dedicated to supporting youth experiencing homelessness, offering emergency shelter, transitional housing, outreach, and employment support. As the region’s primary youth-serving homeless organization, 360° Kids gave C4 teams direct access to the lived realities of young people experiencing homelessness and the service gaps they navigated daily. Their participation in co-design workshops produced the most honest feedback the cohort received — and the most important design pivots.

PARTNER 06

Reena

DEVELOPMENTAL DISABILITIES INCLUSIVE HOUSING COMMUNITY LIVING

Reena is a nonprofit organization in the York Region providing residential, employment, and community support services for adults with developmental disabilities. Their involvement in C4 pushed teams to think beyond “average user” assumptions and design solutions that genuinely serve people across a wide spectrum of abilities, communication styles, and support needs. Reena’s leadership helped the C5 and C2 teams rethink accessibility not as a compliance requirement but as a design aspiration.

PARTNER 07

YorkU Community Safety

CAMPUS SAFETY CRISIS RESPONSE COMMUNITY WELLBEING

York University’s Community Safety team provided an on-campus perspective on how homelessness, mental health, and housing instability present within a post-secondary environment. Their participation grounded the C6 team’s peer support model in a setting where the boundaries between student stress, housing insecurity, and crisis are often invisible. Community Safety also served as a logistics and programme hosting partner, supporting events and sessions held on the Keele campus throughout the year.
