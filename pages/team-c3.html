C3

Resource Access Portal

A live map of every shelter bed, food bank opening, and warming centre — updated in real time by the people who run them.

TEAM PHOTO — PLACEHOLDER
THEME

Project Theme

Service Discovery & Coordination — making the full landscape of community support visible, accurate, and reachable for anyone in need, at any hour.

PROBLEM

Problem Statement

Shelters, food banks, warming centres, and mental health services exist in abundance — but they operate in silos. When someone in crisis tries to find help, they encounter disconnected phone trees, outdated pamphlets, and websites last updated years ago. Referrals fall through the cracks and capacity information is almost never accurate.

WHY

Motivation

People in crisis need accurate information immediately — not a 45-minute phone call to discover a shelter is full. If the infrastructure exists but can’t be found, it might as well not exist. We wanted to make the invisible visible.

CHALLENGE

Biggest Challenge

Aggregating real-time data from dozens of organisations with wildly inconsistent reporting systems. Some organisations update capacity via spreadsheet; others by phone; some not at all. Standardising without imposing new administrative burden was a constant tension.

SOLUTION

Proposed Solution

A live web map and SMS-based service directory with bed-count and capacity indicators, updated directly by service providers through a lightweight admin portal. Organisations receive weekly reminder prompts and a simple one-tap update flow for routine changes.

OUTCOME

Outcome

18 service organisations onboarded across the city. Over 200 resource listings verified and geocoded. Public beta launched with roughly 340 unique users in the first month. Average listing freshness improved from 8 days to under 18 hours.

SDGs

Target SDG Goals

SDG 3 · Good Health & Wellbeing SDG 10 · Reduced Inequalities SDG 11 · Sustainable Cities
LESSON

Most Important Lesson Learned

The technology is the easy part. Sustainable data quality requires genuine organisational buy-in from day one — not just permission to scrape a website. Relationships with service coordinators matter more than any technical architecture we chose.

FUTURE

Future Direction

Build an AI-assisted matching layer that proactively pushes relevant resources to users based on their expressed needs and location. Add multilingual voice search so the portal is usable without literacy or smartphone proficiency. Explore an open API so other organisations can embed the directory.
