Homelessness Through a Management Lens

The C4 Capstone Management Portfolio — York University, 2024–25. Six interdisciplinary teams. Seven community partners. One shared challenge.

PORTFOLIO

Portfolio Title

Homelessness Through a Management Lens: Designing for Community, Housing, and Human Dignity

This portfolio documents the work of the C4 Capstone Management Section at York University. Over eight months, management students coordinated six interdisciplinary Launchpad teams tackling the intersecting causes and consequences of homelessness in the York Region and the greater Toronto area.

PROGRAM

Course & Institutional Context

Program: C4 Launchpad Capstone — Management Section

Institution: York University — Faculty of Liberal Arts & Professional Studies

Academic Year: 2024–2025

C4 is a senior capstone experience in which management students serve as programme managers, strategy leads, and integration architects alongside six interdisciplinary innovation teams. Students apply management theory to real community problems — navigating partners, uncertainty, and the limits of expertise.
