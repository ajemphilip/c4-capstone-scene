Homelessness Through a Management Lens

This portfolio documents the work of the C4 Capstone Management Team — COIL (Section C). Over two weeks, management students coordinated six interdisciplinary Launchpad teams tackling the intersecting causes and consequences of homelessness in Ontario and Canada at large.

TEAMS

Launchpad Teams

C1 The Cost Of Inaction Visualizing the hidden costs of housing insecurity and homelessness. C2 You(th) Are Here The right information, in the right place, for every person who walks through the door. C3 You Belong Bringing residents and organizations into the same room — and keeping them there. C4 Youth Community Connection Fund Hobbies shouldn't cost a youth their shot at belonging. C5 Your Third Space Securing housing is only the beginning, not the finish line. C6 Partner Path The supports exist. They just don't know each other yet.
