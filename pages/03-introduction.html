Introduction

C4 is a senior capstone experience where management students stop studying organisations and start operating inside one — with real partners, real problems, and consequences that extend past the grade.

WHAT IS C4

The Program

C4 (Community, Co-design, Capstone, Collaboration) is York University’s capstone programme for senior management students. Rather than simulated cases, students embed themselves in six interdisciplinary “Launchpad” teams working on live challenges in the homelessness and affordable housing sector. Management students serve as programme managers, strategic coordinators, and process architects — providing the integrative layer that connects six teams into a coherent body of work.

Over the 2024–25 academic year, 30 students from the Faculty of Liberal Arts & Professional Studies worked alongside six interdisciplinary teams, seven community organizations, and hundreds of people with lived experience of homelessness. This portfolio is their collective record.

STRUCTURE

How the Programme Works

The management section operates as the “integration layer” of C4. Each management student is paired with a Launchpad team and serves multiple roles simultaneously: project manager, strategic advisor, and documentation lead. Weekly cross-team management meetings create the synthesis layer that no single Launchpad team can see from within its own project.

The programme runs in four phases: (1) Problem Scoping — immersive partner introductions and field research; (2) Design Sprint — rapid prototyping alongside community members; (3) Pilot — live testing with real end-users; (4) Portfolio — synthesis, reflection, and documentation for handover.
